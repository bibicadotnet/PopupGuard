Privacy Policy for PopupGuard

1. Data Collection
PopupGuard ("the Extension") does not collect, transmit, distribute, or sell any personal data, user data, or web browsing history.

2. Local Storage
The Extension uses your browser's built-in chrome.storage.sync API solely to save your personal preferences (such as your custom allowlists and blocklists). This data is stored locally on your device and synced securely via your logged-in Google account. We do not have access to this data.

3. Third-Party Services
The Extension runs locally on your machine and does not communicate with any external servers, APIs, or third-party tracking services.

4. Contact
If you have any questions regarding this privacy policy, please contact the developer.
